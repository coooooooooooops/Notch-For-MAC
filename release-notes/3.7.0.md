Notch for Mac 3.7.0

New
- Liquid Glass: the tab highlight and Settings cards use Apple's Liquid Glass on macOS 26 (a frosted look on older macOS)
- Fluid motion: the notch opens, closes and resizes on a spring, and the tab highlight glides between tabs
- Themes: Midnight, Aurora, Sunset, Mono and Classic set colours, outline, corners and glass in one tap
- Notch outline: pick the colour and thickness in Settings > Appearance, or turn it off
- Alerts in the pill: volume, display brightness, keyboard light, plugging in or unplugging, low and full battery, and Bluetooth connect or disconnect pop up beside the notch
- Two live activities at once (for example music and a timer) now show side by side
- Optional glass notch body when expanded, and haptic ticks when switching tabs

Fixes
- General improvements
