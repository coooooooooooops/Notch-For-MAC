Notch for Mac 3.5.0

- New Days counter on the Notes tab: create countdowns, give each a name, add as many as you like, and set how many days are left. The number drops by one every day on its own
- Cleaner live activity pill: tidier layout, a progress ring for the focus timer, smoother fade in and out
- The live pill now stays in place when you swipe between desktops, wake your Mac or change displays
- A brief gap in music or timer activity no longer makes the pill blink out
- The Notes tab is a little wider to fit the new card
- Bug fixes and general improvements
