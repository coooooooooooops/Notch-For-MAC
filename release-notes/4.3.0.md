Notch for Mac 4.3.0

New
- The notch grows out of the physical notch: the shape opens on a spring and the tabs are revealed inside it, instead of flashing in
- Full lyrics: click the lyric line (or the lyrics button) to open a scrolling panel with every line sung so far, the current line highlighted, and click any line to jump there
- Lyrics now also work for Safari / YouTube, handle lines with several timestamps, retry with a looser search and fall back to plain lyrics
- Bass booster: switch it on from the music tab or Settings > Sound. Boosts everything the Mac plays on macOS 14.2+ (needs System Audio Recording), or Apple Music's Bass Booster preset otherwise
- Brightness keys: Settings > Alerts can now take over the brightness keys (like volume) so the notch is the brightness indicator. Hold Shift + Option for small steps
- Outline: one even line all the way round, including over the notch itself (switch the top line off in Settings > Appearance)
- Smoother everything: springy button presses, tab icons that pop on hover, tabs that ease in, album-colour glow, numbers that roll, softer toasts and pill

Fixes
- Dictation: switching the mic off now releases the microphone fully (the orange dot goes out) and it is also released when the notch closes
- The notch stays on every desktop, over full-screen apps and when you change apps or windows
- Volume keys no longer get swallowed on outputs that have no software volume
- Lyrics for a song that was not found are retried instead of staying blank
